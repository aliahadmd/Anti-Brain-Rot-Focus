    Quick Guide

    Use Focus in four steps

    Set your distractions once, keep Focus enabled, and let the dashboard track your saves and medals.

    1

    Add blocked sites

    Add domains like youtube.com or use presets in Blocked Sites. Subdomains are covered automatically.

    2

    Keep Focus on

    Use the popup or dashboard switch. A Focus Day counts when Focus stays enabled and you do not pause.

    3

    Check progress

    Analytics shows saves and trends. Rewards shows medal progress and earned awards.

    4

    Pause carefully

    Pausing costs 3 reward days. You will get a warning before the pause is applied.

    Good habits

    • Review Top Distractions when you want to tighten your blocklist.
    • Export your blocklist before moving to another browser profile.
    • Use Clear Pause only when you are ready to return to Focus Mode.
    • Hard delete asks three times so accidental removals are less likely.
